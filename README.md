# claude for GTM 🚀

copy-paste prompts i actually use as a GTM marketer in crypto, fintech and AI.

most marketers use AI as a fancy copywriter. these prompts use it as a researcher, editor, strategist and sparring partner.

> rule #1: AI drafts, you decide. read everything before it goes out.

## the prompts

| # | prompt | use it when |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | [set up your context](prompts/01-context-setup.md) | once, at the start of a project |
| 02 | [clone your voice](prompts/02-voice-clone.md) | your drafts sound like AI, not you |
| 03 | [research before you write](prompts/03-research.md) | you need to know what people actually say about a topic |
| 04 | [one idea → a week of content](prompts/04-repurpose.md) | you have one good post and need 10 more |
| 05 | [the brutal editor](prompts/05-editor.md) | your draft is too long or too corporate |
| 06 | [red-team your launch](prompts/06-red-team-launch.md) | before any launch, campaign or big post |
| 07 | [replies that don't sound like a bot](prompts/07-replies.md) | you want to grow on X through replies |
| 08 | [job hunt on easy mode](prompts/08-job-hunt.md) | applying for GTM / marketing roles |

## how to use
1. start with **01** so claude knows who you are and who you're talking to
2. paste the prompt, fill in the `[brackets]`
3. iterate: "shorter", "punchier", "less salesy", "more like my last post"

## contribute
got a prompt that works for you? open a PR and add it to `/prompts`. keep it practical, no fluff.

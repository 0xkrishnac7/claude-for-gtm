# 08 · job hunt on easy mode

**tailor your CV**
```
here's my CV: [paste]
here's the job description: [paste]

rewrite my CV for this role. keep everything true. reorder and reword to match what they care about.
then list the 3 gaps a hiring manager would notice and how i can address them.
```

**the 3-line DM**
```
write a 3-line DM to [name, role] at [company] about the [role].
line 1: something specific about their product or recent post.
line 2: the one most relevant thing i've done.
line 3: a low-friction ask.
no "i hope this finds you well".
```

**the 90-day plan**
```
i'm interviewing for [role] at [company]. based on their public info, draft a 90-day plan:
first 30 days learn, next 30 days quick wins, last 30 days scale.
make it specific to their product and audience, not generic.
```

**tip:** bring the 90-day plan to the interview. almost nobody does.

# 03 · research before you write

```
i want to write about [topic] for [audience].

before drafting anything:
1. what are people actually saying about this right now? group it into 3-5 camps with their main argument.
2. what do most posts on this get wrong or skip?
3. what questions do beginners keep asking?
4. give me 5 angles nobody seems to be taking.

cite sources where you can. flag anything you're unsure about.
```

**tip:** content built on real conversations beats content built on vibes.

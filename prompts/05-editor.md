# 05 · the brutal editor

my most-used prompt:

```
cut this by 40%. keep my voice. remove anything that sounds like a press release.

[paste draft]
```

variations:
- `make the first line impossible to scroll past. give me 5 options.`
- `what would a skeptical reader roll their eyes at? fix those parts.`
- `rewrite for someone who has never used crypto.`

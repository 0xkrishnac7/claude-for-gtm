# 06 · red-team your launch

```
here's our launch plan / campaign / post:

[paste it]

tear it apart from 4 perspectives:
1. a skeptical degen who has seen 100 launches
2. a compliance officer
3. a busy founder who'd scroll past in 2 seconds
4. a competitor's head of marketing

for each: the biggest weakness, what they'd say publicly, and one fix.
then rank the fixes by impact.
```

**tip:** you'll find the holes before twitter does.

# 07 · replies that don't sound like a bot

```
here are 10 posts from accounts i want to engage with:

[paste posts + author handles]

for each, draft a reply that:
- reacts to their specific point (not a generic "great post!")
- is under 20 words, often under 10
- can be just a reaction or an emoji when that fits better
- is NOT about me or my work unless they literally asked
- sounds like a person typing on their phone

skip any post where there's nothing real to add.
```

**tip:** read every reply before posting. if you wouldn't say it out loud to them, don't post it.
